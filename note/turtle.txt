Turtle Documentation
1. .color() 
    Assign color
2. .goto(0,0)
    Assign position
3. .forward(), .backward(), .left(), .right()
    Moves turtle to specific distance, rotates
4. .clear()
5. .penup()
6. .home()
7. .pendown()
8. .setheading(90) or .seth(90)
    90 degrees Up (North), 
    270 degrees Down (South),
    0 degrees Right (East),
    180 degrees Left (West)
9. .xcor(), .ycor()
    Grabs the coordinates of the turtle
10 .shapesize(stretch_len=0.5, stretch_wid=0.5)
    - Sets the size of the turtle
11. .speed("fastest")
    - Sets the speed 
12. .color()
    - Sets the color of the turtle
13. .write(arg, move=False, align="left", font=("Arial"m 8, "normal"))
    - Writes text on the screen

    n - nonnegative integer
