Turtle Documentation
1. .color() 
    Assign color
2. .goto(0,0)
    Assign position
3. .forward(), .backward(), .left(), .right()
    Moves turtle to specific distance, rotates
4. .clear()
5. .penup()
6. .home()
7. .pendown()
8. .setheading(90) or .seth(90)
    90 degrees Up (North), 
    270 degrees Down (South),
    0 degrees Right (East),
    180 degrees Left (West)
9. .xcor(), .ycor()
    Grabs the coordinates of the turtle



Screen Documentation
1. .onkey(key="", fun=)
    Assign function to key
3. .bgcolor()
    Assign background color
4. .setup(width=, height=)
    Assign dimensions to screen
5. .title()
    Assign title
6. .listen()
    - Listen to keystrokes
7. .tracer(n=None, delay=None)
    n - nonnegative integer
    delay - nonnegative integer
    Turn turtle animation on/off and set delay for update drawings.
